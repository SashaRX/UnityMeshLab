# Source topology and quality-controlled LOD generation

LOD Gen generates every level from working LOD0. Ratios are fractions of LOD0, including the hybrid triangle stage. Each level reports its requested and actual budget, effective error limits, selected candidate and sampled RGBA metrics. No additional native library or plugin ABI change is introduced; library research is in [LOD_TOPOLOGY_RESEARCH.md](LOD_TOPOLOGY_RESEARCH.md).

| Reduction mode | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Triangles | Meshoptimizer with selected UV, normal and float RGBA costs; budget priority by default in LOD Gen, optional strict color validation |
| Full loops from source | Removes complete regular quad loops, stopping when no valid complete operation remains |
| Full loops + triangles | Removes full loops, then attempts the remaining triangle budget; budget priority can fall back to triangle reduction from source |

## Threefold triangle budgets

The planned chain is **LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2**. LOD Gen defaults to two generated levels: fractions 1/3 and 1/9 of working LOD0. LOD2 is the final level and receives the far-profile endpoint. The tool also permits optional additional levels, with stored fractions 1/27 and 1/81, but does not generate them by default. **Reduction per LOD** and **Set reduction each level** rebuild the ratios from the current authored source fraction. Every generated mesh is independently reduced from LOD0.

**Prioritize Triangle Budget** is enabled for Triangles and Full loops + triangles. Triangle reduction now defaults to Balanced quality: three independently reduced source candidates. Fast uses one and High uses five. Each candidate uses native retries with its attribute costs first, then lower costs and higher native error bounds. If necessary, the existing meshoptimizer v1.3 permissive option allows attribute seam collapses. A final retry removes attribute costs; output channels are still copied from retained source vertices. Material slots and Lock Border remain active. No native signature or distributed binary changes are needed.

The chosen result is measured against LOD0. Requested color/normal caps produce warnings rather than rejecting the reduced mesh and restoring a dense source. Generation notes record the selected native error bound, weights and seam relaxation. This prioritizes count, not guaranteed visual quality: thin features can suffer substantially. Locked boundaries, topology and submesh minimums can still prevent a target; missed counts are reported. Full Loops alone retains its complete-loop acceptance rules.

Turn budget priority off to restore strict color validation and safer-budget search. The shared `Options.prioritizeTriangleBudget` defaults to false, preserving existing Prefab Builder caller behavior. The current eight-model LOD0–LOD2 run, its 13-case focused verification, and the earlier extra-level experiment with 73-case targeted verification are recorded in [LOD_PROJECT_VISUAL_EVALUATION.md](LOD_PROJECT_VISUAL_EVALUATION.md).

## Source requirements

Use an FBX exported with original quads, or a working mesh with native Unity quad topology. The FBX SDK path requires the existing FBX Exporter package and `LIGHTMAP_UV_TOOL_FBX_EXPORTER`. Already triangulated sources have no inferred quad pairing and should use Triangles.

Polygon membership and control-point IDs are read directly through the FBX SDK. A temporary FBX copy keeps scale, axis and pivot settings but disables mesh compression before tagged import. Exact corner IDs are bound to the uncompressed import, then to the original compressed import through verified identical indexed connectivity and bounded position quantization. Final correspondence uses the current working mesh, including edits to colors and UV2. Raw topology does not replace the working geometry or attributes with raw coordinates.

Checks cover source corners, triangle coverage, winding, material slots and attribute conflicts. Missing or modified source connectivity is refused before clearing existing generated levels. Imports are cached only for the current Generate action. Both temporary imports are deleted through AssetDatabase on success and failure; original FBX bytes and compression settings are preserved. Read/Write-disabled originals use the existing MeshAccess helper.

## Complete-loop operations

Closed loops pass through regular interior vertices with four incident quads and four neighbors. Open loops may end on suitable boundary vertices. Each complete loop is simulated as one transaction: adjacent quad pairs merge, loop vertices are removed, and replacement faces remain quads. **Lock Border** blocks open loops while permitting interior closed rings.

Acceptance checks oriented manifold edges, vertex fans, boundary loops, connectivity/Euler signatures and detected intersections between replacement and retained triangles. Partial paths, invalid replacements and repeated vertices are rejected. UV, normal, color and tangent discontinuities, material boundaries and creases sharper than 45 degrees protect incident vertices. Triangles, n-gons and irregular poles interrupt regular-loop continuation.

Unchanged faces retain their source triangulation. Replacement quads test both diagonals against source geometry and attributes; invalid/intersecting alternatives are rejected. Accepted local replacements keep their accumulated LOD0 reference triangles so error does not drift through a sequence of removals. The combined replacement is checked again for intersections.

## Quality controls

The following acceptance caps apply with triangle budget priority off and to full-loop operations. In budget-priority triangle output they are diagnostics; native costs/error bounds may be relaxed as described above.

- **Target Error** supplies the native triangle bound and the sampled loop/hybrid weighted error limit. Surface distances are relative to the source bounds diagonal.
- **UV2 Weight, Normal Weight, Color Weight** control attribute costs. Color Weight supports float RGBA, including alpha. Setting it to zero does not disable explicit color validation.
- **Validate Vertex Colors / Max Color Error** limits the maximum sampled absolute difference in any RGBA channel, including alpha. Missing color channels are invalid. Turning the toggle off removes the explicit color cap while retaining native color costs. Existing pipeline callers leaving the color limit unset retain their prior behavior.
- **Max Normal Angle** applies to explicit loop/hybrid validation. Triangle mode uses native Target Error and Normal Weight for its geometry/shading constraints.

**Relax Far LODs** is enabled in LOD Gen by default, beginning at LOD2. Levels before **Relax From LOD** retain base settings. Later levels interpolate toward editable final-level settings. Every native simplification and final validation still refers to working LOD0. With two generated levels, the defaults are:

| Setting | LOD1 | LOD2 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Target Error | 0.20 | 0.30 |
| Normal Weight | 1 | 0.5 |
| Color Weight | 1 | 0.25 |
| Maximum sampled absolute RGBA error | 0.02 | 0.10 |
| Maximum normal angle, loop/hybrid | 15 degrees | 30 degrees |

The panel previews requested settings for every generated level. Turning far relaxation off uses base settings everywhere. A single generated LOD1 uses the base profile. Far-profile relaxation preserves color channels and full-loop protections; triangle budget priority can additionally relax native attribute seams. Neither profile guarantees a particular visual result.

The shared pipeline accepts optional `levelQuality` entries indexed by generated level, validates them before group mutation and reports effective settings. Callers omitting the array, including the existing Prefab Builder path, keep their previous behavior. Reuse of a preceding triangle candidate is disabled when the next native error or attribute constraints are stricter.

## Disconnected small parts

**Remove Small Parts** is an opt-in stage before simplification, available in all three reduction modes. Every level still starts from working LOD0. Original FBX polygon control-point connectivity avoids false components at normal/UV seams; native quads and unavailable FBX mappings use conservative exact-position connectivity. A vertex attachment joins parts; this stage does not cut connected surfaces or recognize semantic objects.

The defaults begin at LOD2, require an estimated size of at most 2 pixels on a 1080-pixel-high screen, and cap cumulative removed source area at 2% and triangles at 20%. Size uses transformed component bounds and the LOD entry transition height relative to LODGroup world size. Summed transformed edge lengths conservatively protect long thin parts. It is a bounds estimate, not visibility, occlusion or a certified silhouette check. Main components, model bounds extrema, zero/invalid-area parts and meshes with deformation channels are retained.

**Analyze Small Parts** lists components and planned removals for the selected preview LOD. Orange Scene View boxes identify planned removals; **Keep part** protects individual components. Preview geometry is checked against the current source; stale manual selections stop removals until refreshed. Analysis and preview do not mutate meshes or scene objects.

Retained surfaces preserve material slots, all UV dimensions, normal/tangent channels, float or byte RGBA and draft UV state. Whole parts are removed from a temporary copy. The final triangle target remains a fraction of original LOD0, and the retained input ratio is adjusted accordingly. Loop source faces are remapped to the retained copy. Temporary retained meshes are destroyed after reduction, and triangle candidates from different retained surfaces are never reused. Explicit geometry/normal/color errors refer to retained LOD0; deleted parts, source triangles, surface area and estimated pixel size are reported separately. GPU captures still compare the complete original model.

The first project evaluation found no removable parts under the default 2-pixel bound. First Aid Kit has 1 component, M84 has 7, Park Bench A has 24, and Park Bench B has 27. The smallest unprotected components of the latter three are approximately 77, 79 and 38 pixels at LOD2 entry. This is intentional retention, not an achieved reduction. See `../_results~/lod-parts-20261009/report.md` for actual component inventories and images.

An explicit 12-pixel LOD4 experiment on Park Bench B removes component 268: 108 source triangles, 0.5521% source area, estimated diameter 9.40 pixels. The same Generate pipeline produces 8,819 triangles versus 8,947 without part removal; both still miss the 584-triangle target. Whole-source silhouette mismatch at 384-pixel capture resolution is 0.0528% front and 0% oblique. This is a limited observed reduction, not multiview acceptance enforcement or proof at gameplay distances. Inputs and graphics are in `../_results~/lod-parts-far-20261009/`.

Verification for this stage: 71 Unity EditMode regression cases passed, including synthetic GPU fixtures, coincident FBX shells, seam duplicates, loss budgets, manual/stale protection, near/far gating, material slots, cancellation and all three generation modes. After adding unreadable-source protection, the focused 14-case component suite passed. The separate four-model GPU evaluation and one-model LOD4 experiment both passed. C# compile checks passed with and without FBX Exporter. These targeted results do not claim the complete package suite is green.

## Candidate selection and color measurement

**Preserve Hard Edges** defaults on in LOD Gen. Other pipeline callers and direct `SimplifySettings` callers opt in through `preserveHardEdges`; their default remains false. This is a strict geometric constraint, separate from Normal Weight and regional normal fitting. Relaxed final-level profiles and permissive zero-cost budget probes do not disable it.

`LodHardEdges` builds exact-position edge adjacency over working LOD0, including cross-material pairs. A discontinuity in either authored endpoint normal identifies a crease (normalized squared difference greater than 1e-10). UV, tangent and RGBA splits alone do not identify hard edges; corner-only contact does not create adjacency. These are current imported/working normals, not original FBX smoothing-group IDs. Missing normals provide no authored-crease evidence. Ambiguous edge incidence, wrong winding and coincident opposite faces are retained conservatively and counted separately; exact coincident geometry cannot recover original control-point identity.

Both incident source triangles form a protected belt. Native simplification receives only the remaining triangles, with `SimplifyLockBorder` enabled whenever a slot has a belt. The remaining target subtracts retained triangles from that slot's requested count, without dropping arbitrary faces when the remainder cannot meet it. The protected triangles are appended with their source positions, winding, corner attributes and material slots; output compaction preserves the ordinary attribute channels and formats. This uses the existing native ABI and distributed binaries, without introducing per-vertex lock marshalling. It is deliberately more conservative than a native selective vertex constraint.

Validation requires original oriented crease segments with authored endpoint normals on both sides, all protected source triangles, and exact oriented interfaces between retained and reduced patches. These checks also run after correction against the retained LOD0 surface if small parts were removed. A failed native check retains an owned source copy; a failed final check similarly retains the source and resets measured diagnostics. Full-loop/hybrid results that change the protected belt are rejected by the final gate. Missed budgets and source fallbacks are reported. A protected LOD can have the same count as the preceding LOD because the feature belt and patch boundaries stop further reduction; this is not ratio reuse.

If the protected face count already reaches/exceeds the requested target, budget-first generation stops the aggressive retry schedule: each quality strategy retains its own costs and requested Target Error. There is no zero-cost/permissive retry or raised error bound. A budget-reaching result still beats every over-budget result; when the protected floor makes the goal unreachable, over-budget strategies rank by measured quality, then triangle count. This avoids deleting unprotected soft parts merely to chase an impossible target. Patch boundaries can impose a stronger floor than retained faces alone; the simple pre-check does not prove a target attainable when it passes.

Protected budget-priority **Triangles** also limits increasing density when a smaller requested target follows an eligible preceding candidate. The existing prior-profile eligibility requires no stricter native error/normal/color costs, and different retained-part surfaces disable reuse. New source-generated strategies at/below the preceding count take precedence over denser strategies. If all are denser, an owned copy of that preceding source-derived geometry becomes an additional reported candidate. Its geometry is not simplified recursively: surface fields are remeasured against LOD0 under the current profile, correction is verified again and the final hard-feature gate still applies. The reported result error for this copy is sampled weighted error, not a new native bound. This fixes the tent's independent quality search selecting 294 triangles after a 284-triangle LOD1.

The constraint preserves detected geometric crease segments, not every thin feature, self-intersection invariant or original material appearance. It does not promise 1/3 or 1/9 attainment. Real models with many discontinuous normals can retain almost all their source triangles. See the hard-edge project evaluation for actual before/after counts and GPU images.

Budget-priority triangle selection compares balanced, shape-emphasis and normal-emphasis candidates; High adds RGBA-emphasis and UV-emphasis candidates. All start from working LOD0 with the same target. A budget-reaching result beats every over-budget result. Ordinarily over-budget strategies prioritize fewer triangles; the protected-floor exception above instead prioritizes measured quality. A candidate in the band target minus max(2 triangles, 5%) through target takes precedence over substantial undershoot; within the same band, measured quality ranks candidates. Fast retains its single candidate. The hybrid source-triangle fallback uses the same selection; full-loop candidate operations retain their own conservative selection below.

Triangle ranking measures six double-sided source-local orthographic silhouette masks at 128 pixels, normalized geometry distance RMS and normal/selected-UV RMS over four equal-area triangle-interior samples in both directions. Existing adaptive RGBA area RMS and all maximum-error diagnostics remain available. The relative score adds distance RMS / 0.01, silhouette mean / 0.05, silhouette maximum / 0.20, normal RMS / max(15 degrees, requested angle) times Normal Weight, maximum channel RGBA RMS / max(0.02, requested color cap) times Color Weight, and protected UV RMS / 0.1 times UV Weight. Far profiles reduce normal/color penalties. These are explicit heuristic scales, not certified acceptance thresholds or a universal visual quality grade. Every candidate's triangles, probe count, score and component errors are retained in diagnostics.

Surface normal computation scales edges before normalization, avoiding Unity's absolute normalization epsilon on very small valid FBX faces. For triangle ranking, zero-area faces are excluded from correspondence on both surfaces; missing nonzero source surface still yields an invalid score. Strict loop/shape validation continues rejecting degenerate faces. Candidate topology is selected using retained source attributes; the optional correction below runs afterward.

Full-loop and hybrid Quality offers Fast (1 variant), Balanced (3), and High (5). Variants start from the same LOD0 with identical limits and weights, varying the greedy objective and deterministic tie order. Hybrid compares complete results rather than intermediate loop meshes. Additional variants can produce identical results and do not guarantee an improvement.

With budget priority off, triangle color validation tries up to 1/3/5 color-weight variants, then up to seven safer budget probes when the best validated candidate misses the target. Every probe simplifies LOD0. A preceding validated candidate may be cloned and revalidated against LOD0 without recursive simplification, preventing later levels from becoming denser merely because independent searches found different candidates. A source copy is retained only when no reduced candidate passes; the report explicitly marks missed budgets and counts all validation attempts.

Color checks sample vertices, edges and face interiors in both surface directions. Maximum absolute errors are reported per RGBA channel; RMS is weighted by surface area. Interior sampling increases for gradients. One-sided spatial correspondence handles seams without choosing a triangle by color agreement; identical positions/connectivity use known face correspondence. Changed attributes are still measured. Rejected candidates can stop sampling early after exceeding a cap, while accepted candidates retain complete bidirectional metrics.

## Verification and limits

**Correct Normals / RGBA** defaults on in LOD Gen's budget triangle path, including the hybrid source-triangle fallback. Successful full-loop reductions and legacy callers do not use this correction. `Options.correctSurfaceAttributes` defaults false for other callers. Correction fits fields over the selected geometry against working LOD0 (or retained LOD0 after optional part removal), independently for each level; it never changes positions, indices, material slots or UVs.

Nine equal-area centroids per triangle in both projection directions contribute to sparse barycentric least squares. A source-value prior limits drift; the final far profile allows weaker regularization. Correspondence is geometry/winding-based, restricted to the same material slot, and shares the validator's one-sided seam handling. No position welding is performed. Existing duplicated hard color/normal seams and vertices shared across material slots are pinned. This cannot recreate seams already removed by budget-first simplification.

Equal-color render duplicates share one RGBA variable only across unambiguous adjacent edges within a material slot. Unlinked coincident copies, corner contacts, ambiguous edges and material boundaries stay conservatively pinned. If every ordinary color-fit strength fails acceptance, varying-color fields try area-weighted source redistribution using the same barycentric samples. A variable's accumulated source RGBA is divided by its sample mass; pinned variables keep their prior. Full/half/quarter blends use the same quantized per-channel RMS/maximum gate. Already accepted ordinary fits and constant fields avoid these extra trials. The selected method is reported; this fallback changes no geometry and does not guarantee categorical paint boundaries.

Since 2026-10-10, normal correction additionally derives connected smoothing regions from working LOD0. A pair of oppositely oriented edges with exactly matching positions and matching endpoint normal directions (squared normalized difference at most 1e-10) establishes continuity across render-vertex splits. UVs, colors and tangents do not split these normal regions. Material slots, discontinuous endpoint normals, corner-only contacts, same-direction edges, more than two incident faces and coincident opposite triangles do not establish continuity. These are inferred normal-continuous regions, not recovered FBX/DCC smoothing-group bitmasks; ambiguous spatial connectivity remains conservative and cannot identify every coincident-shell configuration without original control-point provenance.

Four geometry/winding-based face-interior probes associate each output triangle with one source region. A mixed/unmapped face pins its vertices; a vertex shared by different source regions is also pinned. Normal fitting uses source/destination surfaces restricted to that association in both directions. Output edge adjacencies within the same source region link duplicate render vertices into one fitting variable only when geometry-based source projections from both faces have continuous endpoint normals. This can repair a transfer-created mismatch across a smooth UV seam without treating that mismatch as an authored hard edge. The mesh vertex/index layout and UV seams remain untouched. A pin or invalid hemisphere trial applies to all copies of that variable. RGBA continues using its independent fit and seam protections.

Each region has a cached bidirectional verification correspondence: four area-weighted interior centroids estimate angular RMS, with vertices and edge midpoints contributing to the maximum. In addition to the global normal gate, **no measured region's RMS or maximum may increase** beyond 0.0001 degrees relative to the baseline or the preceding accepted trial. Regional correspondence is fixed before fitting and never selected by normal agreement. Incomplete correspondences pin the affected region; source regions with no mapped output face, mixed faces and linked duplicates are reported explicitly. Missing-region diagnostics do not claim preservation or repair. Interior probes can miss small crossed regions, and a connected region label does not encode every internal crease; these checks do not turn budget-first collapse into strict feature preservation.

Full, half and quarter-strength fits are verified against LOD0 before acceptance. Authored normal area RMS must improve and authored maximum angle must not increase (numerical tolerance 0.0001 degrees). The authored maximum is tracked separately from the geometric face maximum, so a large geometric error cannot hide worse shading normals. For RGBA, no channel's sampled maximum or area RMS may increase (tolerance 0.000001), and at least one channel's RMS must improve. Alpha is checked independently. Rejected fits keep their original channel. Verification uses different normal quadrature from fitting; these are sampled checks, not certified bounds or an independent holdout dataset.

Float/HDR colors retain their channel format and are bounded by each fitted vertex's original and sampled local range, rather than clamped to 0..1 or converted to sRGB. Color32 changes are measured after quantization. Accepted normals are normalized in their original hemisphere, and tangent XYZ is orthogonalized while preserving handedness. Missing channels are not invented. Deforming meshes skip correction; temporary fitting clones are destroyed on rejection/cancellation.
